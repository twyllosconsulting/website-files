Who We Are

Built for Africa's Most Ambitious Organisations

Twyllos Consulting Limited is a premier management and strategy consulting firm delivering integrated advisory services across project management, stakeholder engagement, policy development, and business growth.

Founded on the conviction that every organisation holds untapped potential, we bring together senior-level expertise and deep sectoral knowledge to unlock measurable value for clients across the public, private, and development sectors.

With a multidisciplinary team of seasoned consultants and a rigorous, evidence-based approach, we partner with organisations at every stage of their transformation journey — from strategy design to full-scale implementation and post-project evaluation.
